Branding

MEADOW

Industry: Beauty

Meadow is a skincare brand that embodies the elegance of nature and the purity of organic beauty. A delicate golden floral emblem graces the logo, symbolizing growth, renewal, and the richness of botanical ingredients.

The typography is refined and graceful, mirroring the brand’s commitment to high-quality, nature-inspired skincare. Every detail, from the elegant packaging to the rich textures, is designed to create an indulgent self-care experience. Meadow is more than skincare—it’s a ritual, a moment of connection with the natural world, wrapped in sophistication.

YONDER

Industry: Bookstore

Yonder is a bookstore dedicated to book lovers and creative minds. A stylized open book with flowing pages, a warm and earthy color palette, and high-quality imagery celebrating the joy of reading.

The typography is elegant and timeless, reflecting the brand’s dedication to literature and knowledge. The branding exudes a sense of curiosity, imagination, and sophistication. The use of deep wood tones, soft lighting, and vintage-inspired design elements creates a cozy and inspiring atmosphere, making Yonder a haven for readers, writers, and dreamers alike.
